What It Does
ANNOTATE SHOP FLOOR PHOTOS FOR 5S LEAN DOCUMENTATION
⚠️
Hazard Tape Lines
Draw black/yellow, red/white, and other 5S standard stripe patterns directly on photos. Multi-point chains with clean mitered corners — looks like real floor tape.
⬡
Zone Marking
Tap multiple points to define any zone shape. Semi-transparent fill with 5S standard colors — yellow for lanes, green for finished goods, red for quarantine.
🔲
Shadow Boards
Trace tool outlines manually, or use the AI Detect button to automatically identify tools and generate shadow board outlines from a photo.
📋
Audit Reports
Built-in 5S scoring (1–5 per S, out of 25). Generate a professional HTML/PDF report with annotated photo, action table, audit scores, and findings.
✥
Move · Resize · Rotate
Select any annotation after placing it. Drag to move, use blue handles to resize individual points, orange handle to rotate the whole shape.
🗺
Auto Legend
Toggle a live color key that shows only the colors you've actually used — exports with the photo so anyone can read your annotations without explanation.

How It Works
NO ACCOUNT · NO INSTALL · RUNS IN YOUR BROWSER
1
Load Your Photo
Tap "Load Photo" to pick from your camera roll, or use the camera button to shoot directly. Supports HEIC, JPEG, PNG — whatever your iPhone takes.
2
Pick a Tool and Annotate
Select Hazard Tape, Zone, Arrow, Label, or any other tool from the bottom bar. Tap points on the photo to draw — double-tap or press ✓ to finish shapes. Pinch to zoom for precision.
3
Adjust With Move Mode
Tap ✥ MOVE to select, reposition, resize, or rotate any annotation. Duplicate with ⎘ DUP. Double-tap labels to edit text. Adjust opacity live.
4
Score the Audit
Tap 📊 to open the 5S Audit panel. Rate each S from 1–5, add notes per category, get a score out of 25 with letter grade.
5
Export Your Report
Save the annotated photo (with legend overlay) to your Photos app. Or tap 📋 to generate a full PDF audit report with photo, annotation table, audit scores, and action columns.
Install as an App

Mac / PC
