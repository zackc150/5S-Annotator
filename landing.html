What It Does
Everything you need for professional 5S visual documentation
⚠️
Hazard Tape & Solid Tape
Draw black/yellow, red/white, and 6 other stripe patterns directly on photos. Solid color tape in all 5S standard colors. Multi-point chains with clean mitered corners and an optional CAD-style centerline.
📐
Real-World Measurements
Calibrate scale by tapping two known points. Every annotation automatically shows real dimensions — line lengths, zone areas in sq ft, circle diameters. CAD-style dimension lines with arrows. Dimensions export with the photo and appear in reports.
📋
Audit Reports & Scoring
Built-in 5S scoring (1–5 per S, out of 25, letter grade A–F). Generate a professional HTML report with annotated photo, action table, audit scores, real dimensions, and annotation notes. Print as PDF from any browser.
💾
Save & Restore Sessions
Export your entire work session — photo, all annotations, measurement scale, and audit scores — to a .5ss file. Reload it later to continue exactly where you left off. Works across devices.
✥
Full Edit Mode
Select any annotation to move, resize, rotate, duplicate, lock, or delete it. Copy style from one annotation to paste onto others. Control z-order. Adjust opacity live. Add notes to individual annotations.
🗺
Auto Legend
Toggle a live color key showing only the colors you've used with their 5S standard meanings. Exports baked into the saved photo so anyone can read your annotations without explanation.
🧲
Smart Snap System
Snaps to endpoints, midpoints of existing lines, and auto-locks to 0°/45°/90° angles like CAD software. Auto-detects when you're closing a shape. Toggle snap on/off anytime.
🤖
AI Shadow Board Detection
Point the camera at a tool board and tap AI Detect — Claude Vision automatically identifies each tool and draws shadow board outlines around them. One tap, ready to print.

How It Works
No account · No install · Runs in your browser
1
Load Your Photo
Tap PHOTO to pick from your camera roll, or tap CAMERA to shoot directly. Supports HEIC, JPEG, PNG. Photo never leaves your device.
2
Calibrate Scale (Optional)
Tap MEASURE, then tap two points you know the real distance between. Enter the distance — every annotation now shows real-world dimensions automatically.
3
Draw Your Annotations
Pick a tool from the bottom bar and tap points on the photo. Snap system locks to nearby points and 45° angles. Double-tap or ✓ to finish shapes. The app shows you exactly what you're drawing in real time.
4
Edit and Refine
Switch to Edit mode to move, resize, rotate, and duplicate anything. Copy styles across annotations, add notes, adjust opacity, lock annotations in place so they can't be accidentally moved.
5
Run the Audit
Tap AUDIT to score each S from 1–5 with notes. Get a score out of 25 with a letter grade. Export the audit as a standalone HTML file.
6
Export Everything
SAVE exports the annotated photo to your camera roll (iPhone) or downloads it (Mac). REPORT generates a professional PDF-ready HTML report with photo, all annotations, dimensions, notes, and audit score. SESSION saves your entire work to reload later.
Install as an App
Works offline · No App Store needed
📱
iPhone / iPad
Open in Safari → tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Runs fullscreen like a native app. Works offline after first visit. Supports HEIC photos natively.
💻
Mac / PC
Open in Chrome or Edge → click the install icon in the address bar. Installs as a desktop app. Full keyboard shortcuts: ⌘Z undo, D/E/V modes, Enter to finish shapes, F for fullscreen.
🔒
Private by Default
Your photos never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Sessions are saved as files you control.
